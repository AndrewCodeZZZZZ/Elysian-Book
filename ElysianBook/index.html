A little story begins

Chapter I

A little introduction

Hello.

I wanted to write something a little different instead of simply sending another message.

So I made this little book.

And I suppose this is where my story begins.

01

Chapter II

A beautiful coincidence

Somewhere along the way, I came across your cosplay.

And honestly, Elysia was what first caught my attention.

There was something about seeing a character I already admired brought to life.

It made me curious enough to stay a little longer.

02

Chapter III

Beyond the cosplay

But after a while, I realized something.

It wasn't just the character that made me curious.

I started wondering about the person behind the cosplay.

The person with her own stories, interests, little habits, and dreams.

And I found myself wanting to know more about you.

03

Chapter IV

A little honesty

I think you're really lovely.

And somewhere between admiring your work and becoming curious about you, I realized that my interest had become something more.

I didn't want to pretend that I hadn't noticed.

So I thought I'd be honest about it.

04

Chapter V

A small question

I don't know where this might lead.

And I don't want to make any assumptions about you.

But if you're comfortable with it, I'd really like the chance to get to know you.

Maybe we could talk, share our interests, and see where things go.

No expectations. Just a genuine invitation.

05

The final page

A letter from me

Thank you for taking the time to read this little book.

I know this might be unexpected, and I hope it doesn't make things uncomfortable.

I simply wanted to tell you how I feel, and ask if you'd be open to getting to know me.

Whatever your answer is, I'll respect it.

And either way, I'm glad I got to admire your beautiful work.

With sincerity,
Someone who wanted to say hello

Would you be open to getting to know me?
